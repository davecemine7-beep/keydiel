PageTurner Bookshop

An online bookstore where readers can browse books, search by title or author, and order with cash on delivery, GCash, or card.

  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
Problem
Readers needed a simple way to find books and order them online, with payment options that fit how Filipinos actually pay.
Built
A bookstore website with a home page, search, a shelf of 18 titles, a cart, sign-up and log-in, and checkout.
Role
Developer and designer (solo project).
How
Built in PHP on a local server, with a warm brown-and-cream design that keeps the books front and center.
Result
A working site where customers can browse the catalog and choose from four ways to pay, including cash on delivery.
Learned
Planning the pages and features before coding saved time. Next, I would add an admin dashboard for managing books and orders, and publish the site online.
